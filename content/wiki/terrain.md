## Slope and contours

Close contour lines mean steep ground: downhill accelerates you, uphill slows you a little, and following a contour keeps nearly full cruise. Boost stacks with downhill speed, but either ship can climb without it. Ordinary terrain does no damage and does not deflect shots.

## Outer boundary

The circular wall destroys a vulnerable ship on contact. Rocks bounce inward, and reflected lasers become dangerous ricochets.

## Spider nests

Red map webs mark discovered guarded resources; the mark disappears when the resource is collected or moved. Guards chase briefly and return home, while rare roaming hunters pursue farther. Red contours and a red screen edge warn of nearby spiders and pursuit.

## Survive a hunt

One bite kills, so shoot the spider, escape its range, or reach a lit furnace's safe area. Mineral Scan repels spiders while they remain in range; probes and dark foundations offer no protection. Killing guards clears that nest for the server session, but collecting its resource does not remove surviving guards.

## Spider tools

Haulers can extract silk or tow a spider into a furnace, although towing calls rescuers that can cut the cable. Surveyors can attach a probe to follow a guard home to its resource. See the [Hauler](/wiki/#hauler) and [Surveyor](/wiki/#surveyor) tools.
