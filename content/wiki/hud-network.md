## Probe beacons

A pulsing ring on the minimap is a live Survey Probe. Follow that rock.

## What the HUD shows

Lives, score, and the local radar sit on the playfield. M opens the universe map. Sound, music, and haptics are on the title screen. Haptics buzz your own hits on phones that can vibrate. Advanced Debug copies a Player ID for a bug report.

## Display refresh rate

A faster screen does not speed the ship. If your prediction drifts, the server puts you back on the accepted path.

## Connection interruptions

The game reconnects on its own. A short drop returns you to the same ship. Stay away longer and Enter Game starts a new flight with the score you still have.

## Surveyor scan

Press E and nearby rocks show their minerals on every radar. Uncharted ground stays hidden until someone flies there.
