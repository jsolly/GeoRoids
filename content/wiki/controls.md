## North-up view

The viewport and maps keep north at the top while your ship turns. Point or drag toward a direction to steer toward it; holding the pointer still keeps that target direction, and pointing at or very near your own hull stops the turn. Drift and knockback can carry the ship sideways without rotating the view.

## Keyboard

Steer with A/D or Left/Right, hold Space to fire, hold F and release for a charged beam, and press E for your equipped tool. Hold W or Up to thrust; release to brake. There is no automatic thrust; reaching a lit furnace ring with cargo stops either ship to [offload](/wiki/#loot-growth) until the hold empties or you steer away. Shift toggles Contour Lock; [ and ] hop toward lower and higher neighboring contours while settled on a line.

## Mouse

Move the pointer to steer toward it, click to fire (each click is one shot; holding does not repeat), and right-click to toggle Contour Lock. Keyboard steering takes over until you move the pointer again.

## Touch

Tap a visible destination to fly there and brake to rest; tap elsewhere to redirect, or use Stop to cancel. A dotted journey line shows your destination and marks its first obstructing asteroid, but does not avoid obstacles. Scrapes preserve the order; sustained blockage cancels it.

Tap Fire for one shot; hold to charge and release for a beam. Drag from Fire to cancel travel, brake, and aim while charging, or drag from your ship to turn without moving. Two fingers on the playfield activate a two-second shield with a twelve-second cooldown; Fire-button touches do not count toward this gesture.

Swipe to use your equipped ability, or tap its cooldown indicator. While contour travelling, swipes switch isolines instead; the indicator still activates your tool. Leaving the tab or app cancels travel and held input.

Hold a visible object to open its actions, then release over an action or tap one; Cancel dismisses the menu. A faint glow identifies objects with actions. Inspect works immediately; Signal crew is available within signalling range on campaign servers. Furnace actions approach into range first and recheck eligibility. Nearby furnaces also offer action pop-ups before your ship overlaps their footprint.

## Contour Lock

Near a visible contour, tap Contour Lock to catch it and follow its curves at extra speed. The lock follows the direction your nose points, with distinct chimes when it engages or releases. Tap the same control again to release; steering is ignored while locked. Damage, a hard shove, the world wall, asteroid contact (even while protected), opening a menu or map, death, and furnace travel also release it, and you cannot lock during knockback or while a menu is open. Lasers pass through invulnerable hulls. There is no charge or cooldown, but the button needs a nearby contour. A connection interruption releases the lock; reacquire it after reconnecting.

## Mobile contour hopping

While settled in Contour Lock, flick back along your line to reverse, or flick toward an adjacent line to glide onto it; a diagonal flick chooses your new travel direction. Use one finger, move at least 24 screen pixels within 220 milliseconds, and release briskly. Make at most three adjacent hops without a two-second pause; the third leaves a [crew bridge](/wiki/#terrain). A rising chime confirms a successful change and a softer falling chime marks a blocked attempt; flicks reach a little farther than ordinary lock pickup, but unreachable lines and flicks made while still joining a line leave your route unchanged.

## Contour travel

Follow the gray lines for extra speed in either direction; crossing them keeps normal cruise. Contour Lock follows a nearby line for stronger speed. See [Contour travel](/wiki/#terrain).

## Inventory and menus

V or Inventory opens your ship schematic to choose [owned tools](/wiki/#loot-growth) and equip [satellites](/wiki/#satellites). Map, Inventory, and the furnace menu hold your ship safely while the world keeps moving; satellite health still drains and you cannot collect pickups. Your ship stops dead and drops Contour Lock. Close with Escape or the close control to resume flight with brief blink protection. Return to flight also restores keyboard focus to the playfield. Each join starts with Tow Cable or Mineral Scan.

## Furnace travel and store

On desktop, inside a lit furnace's visible footprint, press E or use the travel prompt to open the destination map (on desktop E opens the menu instead of your tool there; fly off the footprint to use the tool); B also toggles the menu. At Town Square, tap Enter or press E, then choose Store or Fast Travel; Back to Town Square returns to that choice. On mobile, long press any visible furnace or use its nearby pop-up to approach and enter; the tappable ability indicator keeps your equipped tool available.

Pinch the destination map to zoom in or out, and drag to explore. Keyboard users can focus the map and use +/− to zoom. Choose another lit furnace for a free ride along the pipes in your equipped ship; dark foundations are not destinations. Boarding releases your tow or armed coupling, and you cannot steer, fire, change tools, collect loot, or take damage during the ride.

Town Square's Store offers placeholders purchased with banked points. Higher settlement levels unlock more offers; each placeholder can be bought once and has no gameplay effect.

## Map

M or Map opens the shared universe chart centred on your ship. Drag to pan and pinch to zoom; desktop also supports arrow keys, the mouse wheel, and +/− keys. Use Center on you or Home to restore the nearby ship view. M, Escape, or Close returns to flight.

## Keyboard access and motion

Tab reaches menu controls and the touch tool and Contour Lock buttons; Enter or Space activates a focused button. Focus the universe chart itself for arrow-key panning and +/− zoom, because a focused button keeps its normal keyboard behavior. On the furnace destination map, Tab selects a lit stop and Enter chooses it.

Your device's reduced-motion setting skips the opening camera dive, steadies prominent loot and travel flames, and replaces Wiki animations with still images. The game still moves in real time. The universe chart also exposes a text list of revealed landmarks and crew coordinates for assistive technology.

## Campaign controls

Use Help here or Nest here to signal nearby crew; Shift-click Map on desktop or tap without dragging on touch to place a help signal. With a keyboard, pan with the arrow keys, then choose Signal help at center or press Shift+Enter on the chart; signals reach nearby crew only. Hold Revive near a downed pilot, or activate it with a keyboard or assistive control to start or cancel; see [rescue rules](/wiki/#combat-survival). At a stocked furnace, a Hauler with Tow Cable can choose Tow supply for [frontier restoration](/wiki/#teamwork).
