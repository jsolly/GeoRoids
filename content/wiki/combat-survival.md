## Firing while moving

Shots inherit your ship's motion; hold Space to repeat them, while a mouse click fires once. Direct crew shots mine rocks, kill spiders, and damage AI Raiders without hurting friendly pilots or AI Workers. After a wall, reflective-rock, or court-panel bounce, a shot becomes a dangerous ricochet that can hit anyone.

## AI Raiders

Red ships named AI Raider pursue nearby pilots and workers and fire ordinary damaging bolts. Shoot them directly, or return to the Town Square sanctuary where raider bolts cannot hurt you; normal protection still applies outside town. Raiders respawn and carry no kill bounty.

## Charged beam

Hold Fire on mobile, or F on desktop, then release for a larger, stronger shot; a growing ring shows the charge. Longer holds increase mining damage up to a fixed maximum, followed by a short charge cooldown; a quick tap on Fire shoots an ordinary bolt. Opening a menu, losing focus, cancelling a touch, or dying cancels the charge, and abandoned holds expire.

## Ricochet Court

Meet at the cyan court northeast of Town Square for informal bank-shot duels; it stays marked on both maps before exploration. Its indestructible panels reflect lasers without changing their energy, while ships pass through safely. Banked shots can escape through the gaps and hurt anyone, with normal death and protection rules and no separate duel score.

## Damage and protection

Hazards spill [cargo](/wiki/#loot-growth) before damaging your hull; with an empty hold, the outer wall and a spider bite can kill outright. Spawn blinking and open map, schematic, or store menus protect your hull. A vulnerable ship that rams an ordinary rock also breaks it, leaving its points and a shard, while a protected ship passes through unharmed. Ship-to-ship ramming, tow cables, and loot blasts do not hurt crew hulls.

## Lives and respawn

Death loses your carried points. You respawn at Town Square with restored health and brief protection; banked points, salvaged tools, and silk remain safe. Death also releases any tow and drops satellites. There are no lives or game over. Returning after a brief disconnect skips spawn protection.

## Recovery

Avoid damage for a short time and your hull repairs itself automatically. A hit that removes health or spills protective cargo restarts the wait; repairs stop at full health and never revive a destroyed ship. Bank cargo at a lit furnace, then spend banked points on placeholders at [Town Square](/wiki/#controls).

## Downed and rescue

On a campaign-enabled server, a lethal hit leaves you downed with a rescue beacon and a countdown. Another live pilot can hold Revive beside you, or activate it with a keyboard or assistive control to start or cancel; moving, damage, releasing a pointer hold, opening an overlay or either pilot disconnecting cancels the attempt. A completed rescue restores part of your hull with brief protection; Give up or an expired countdown causes the ordinary death and cargo loss, even if you reconnect after the deadline.

## Hull deployment

Your hull pieces snap together on deployment and respawn while flight controls remain available. Reduced motion skips the assembly.

## Timed shield

Press two fingers on the playfield, or tap Shield, for two seconds of damage protection. The twelve-second cooldown starts on activation; pressing again cannot extend protection. Collisions can still shove your ship, and firing and travel continue.
