## What the manual covers

Surveyor scouts and shares scans. Hauler taps, tows, or launches rocks to Town Square. The chart and delivered ground stay between sessions.

## Start a life

Pick a ship and press Enter Game. Score lasts through death and a short disconnect. Game over starts over at three lives and score 0. Mass resets every life.
