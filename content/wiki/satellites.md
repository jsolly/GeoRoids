## Six Earth-observation hulls

Landsat 7, Terra, Aqua, GOES-16, ENVISAT, and WorldView-3 sit in the field and glow until someone collects them.

## Shared pickup rules

Loose satellites do not take damage. Fly close and the nearest living ship claims one.

## Inventory

Collected hulls wait in the schematic. They do not orbit or decay until you equip one.

## Equipped satellites

Equip one satellite. It identifies nearby rocks, then its green bar drains with time and hits. At empty it is gone. Death drops what you were carrying.

## Satellite audio

Your own satellite chimes once each orbit. Sound Effects off stops it.
