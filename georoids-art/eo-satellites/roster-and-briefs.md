## Per-bird art notes (compressed)

### Landsat 7

Short fat cylinder bus; one 4-panel wing longer than bus (~8.5 m vs 4.3 m). 3/4 from array side. Gold/yellow bus + blue array ok as phosphor tints.

### Terra

School-bus body; **sail** blanket (not paneled paddle); MISR barrel bank on side. No dual wings, no dish.

### Aqua

Dish is the tell — if dish isn’t obvious at play scale, drop Aqua. 3/4 dish toward camera + long array spine.

### GOES-16

Asymmetric: giant 5-panel wing vs thin mag boom (~8 m). Fat Earth-face telescope. Never top-down.

### ENVISAT

Biggest sprite. Solar wall + SAR plank as two big rectangles on different axes. Never hide the plank.

### WorldView-3

Taller than wide; modest dual arrays. Side/3/4 for height. Never top-down.

## Don’t

- UFO saucer disc (temp SVG is temp only)
- Top-down for GOES or WV-3
- Hide Aqua dish or ENVISAT SAR
- Full-hull faction paint on NPCs
