## Mineral scan

Press E to classify nearby rocks on every teammate's radar. The classification sticks, spiders inside the scan flee, and a later furnace delivery pays you too.

## Survey probe

Fire a beacon onto a rock or spider. It keeps identifying nearby materials until the battery dies or someone shoots it off. A miss does not start the cooldown.

## Satellite inventory

Pickups wait in the schematic. Equip one to scan nearby rocks. It drains with time and hits, and your E ability stays yours.
