## Read the landscape

Follow the lines for an easy cruise. Downhill is fast and uphill is a little slow. The ground itself does not hurt you. Crossing the outer wall costs a life.

## Slope and contours

Tight lines are steep. Turn downhill and speed more than doubles. Boost stacks with that descent.

## Terrain spiders

Some resources have a nest, shown as a red web on the map. One bite is fatal. Shoot them, scan them away, or tow one into Town Square.
