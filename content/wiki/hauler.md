## Utility slot

Open the schematic and equip Resource Tap, Tow Cable, or Boost Coupling. E fires whichever tool is mounted.

## Resource Tap E

E pulls canisters from a nearby rock, or silk from a spider, and leaves the target whole. Press E again to stop. Silk has no use yet.

## Boost Coupling E

The first E arms a rock toward Town Square. The second E, or IGNITE on touch, sends it. A colossal rock needs two couplings. The grate shatters it and pays you and any Surveyor who scanned it.

## Tow cable E

E attaches a tow cable to the nearest living asteroid or spider, and the catch trails behind you. The cable never reels a rock into the hull or throws it. Fly the rock into Town Square to deliver it. Ordinary cargo breaks on another rock or ship. A colossal deposit needs two cables.

## Mining lasers

Hauler shots hit metal and huge rocks harder. They never damage a teammate.

## Satellite inventory

Equip one stored satellite from the schematic. Tow, tap, and boost stay available.
