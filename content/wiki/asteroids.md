## Field and materials

Rocks fill the world and stay gone once mined. Ice and rubble break easier than metal. A rare colossal deposit needs two Haulers or many shots.

## Self-powered rocks

An armed coupling shows a cream arrow. After ignition the rock flies itself to Town Square and ignores everything until the grate takes it.

## Cooperative splits and score

Two different pilots hitting one large rock split it and shove the field. The same pilot, or a late second hit, just breaks it. Everyone who mined or scanned the rock shares the score.

## Reflection and charge

Metal faces bounce your shot and charge the rock. After a bounce, that shot can hit a ship.
