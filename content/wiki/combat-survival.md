## Firing while moving

Shots leave the nose and inherit your speed. A bounced shot can hit a ship. A direct shot does not hurt a teammate.

## Damage and protection

An asteroid impact takes a chunk of health and you keep flying. The outer wall does not. The map, schematic, and store let the world pass through you until you close them.

## Lives and respawn

You start with three lives. Death spends one and returns you beside Town Square with a fresh hull and a blink. The last life is game over.

## Score values

Breaks, pickups, and furnace deliveries add score. That score stays until game over.
