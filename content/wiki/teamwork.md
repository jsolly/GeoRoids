## One crew

Human pilots fly on the same team, with informal duels at the [Ricochet Court](/wiki/#combat-survival). Direct lasers and ship collisions are safe, but ricochets and towed rocks can hurt teammates.

## AI Workers

Green ships named AI Worker collect nearby loose point cargo and return it to a lit furnace, contributing to the shared settlement rather than a pilot's bank. Between jobs they hang back in your general vicinity with loiter targets safely inside the world boundary; they leave equipment, silk, and satellites for you, so protect them from [AI Raiders](/wiki/#combat-survival). The world has one worker and one raider while pilots are connected; workers need a lit furnace near the crew to offload.

## Scan, tow, deliver

Scan a rock before a Hauler tows or boosts it into a lit furnace. The delivering Haulers and recorded scanning pilots each earn rewards; each contributor receives the same base reward. Bring two Haulers for a colossal deposit.

## Leapfrog on contours

Follow a teammate's line through the bright bridge left by their third quick hop to glide onto the next line with a brief drafting boost. Your crossing keeps the connection open for the next pilot; each pilot gets one use, and the connection has a fixed maximum lifetime. See [cooperative hops](/wiki/#terrain).

## Shared chart

Every pilot reveals part of the persistent crew map, with Scout covering more ground. Discovered resources and furnaces help teammates find work beyond their local radar. Open M or Map to plan a route together.

## Named furnaces

Town Square starts lit; Scouts spend score to build outward through dark furnace foundations, and a Hauler crossing one is reminded that only Scouts can build. A lit furnace gains a fire trail back to the square and becomes a shared delivery site and spider refuge. See [furnace travel and store](/wiki/#controls) to ride your ship between lit stops or buy placeholders at Town Square.

## Persistent world

Exploration, built furnaces, and the asteroid field survive server restarts until the expedition resets. Harvested deposits regrow out of sight, so a mined field is worth revisiting.

## Build the settlement

Everyone shares settlement progress. Reaching the next level requires deposited points and four materials, each scaling with the current level (per-level amounts are in the reference below). Deposits and every contributor's delivery reward count toward it. All requirements must be met; the recipe is consumed and surplus carries forward. The central docking station grows as the settlement levels up. Higher levels unlock more store placeholders; these spend banked points but grant no upgrade or gameplay effect.

## Escort carriers

A loaded carrier draws nearby spiders. Escort the carrier and clear its route to a lit furnace.

## Crew signals and rescue

On a campaign-enabled server, use Help here or Nest here; Shift-click the chart, or tap it on touch, to place a nearby help signal. Downed pilots automatically broadcast a rescue beacon; hold Revive beside one, or activate it with a keyboard or assistive control to start or cancel the countdown; choose Give up to respawn. See [survival](/wiki/#combat-survival) for interruption and reconnect rules.

## Restore the frontier

Clear a queen to expose her furnace ruin, then haul physical supply from a lit settlement closer to Town Square. Deliver ore to stock a settlement, select Tow Cable, and use Tow supply while inside its intake; carry the shipment outward to the ruin. Finishing construction permanently lights the furnace and charts its surroundings; surplus becomes stock there, while destroyed shipments lose their supply.

## Deep Scanner

Physical ore delivered to Town Square also fills the shared Deep Scanner project while keeping ordinary contributor rewards. Finishing it reveals the outer hive frontier to everyone; banked points cannot fund this project or a frontier ruin. These campaign systems appear only after the server's reviewed world migration is activated.
