Skirmisher's E burst count is three; normal firing remains one laser per
trigger.

## Burst fire

E fires a three-shot burst with a narrow spread and keeps the ability active for
8 frames. The shared laser cap still applies, so existing lasers can limit how
many projectiles the burst creates. E follows the common ability guards for
exploding, dead, cooldown, and asteroid-motion states.
