## One crew

Everyone shares the field. Direct shots and rams do not hurt teammates. A ricochet can.

## Scan, tow, deliver

A Surveyor marks a rock. A Hauler brings it to Town Square. Both are paid. The store there sells extra lives.

## Shared chart

Both maps share one explored chart. Surveyors reveal farther than Haulers. Town Square stays marked on the universe map.

## Harvested ground

Mined regions stay empty and still flyable. A new flight starts on the ring around Town Square.
