# Parked ideas

and Todoist tasks are closed as cancelled; implementation requires a new request.

## Rune-etched asteroids

Original proposal: [GitHub issue #496](https://github.com/jsolly/GeoRoids/issues/496).
Former task: [Todoist](https://app.todoist.com/app/task/6hRXX2qmggPhjvxR).

- Etch faction-specific tactical symbols onto asteroid surfaces.
- Let the owning faction read its marks immediately; let opponents learn the
  current codex by scanning distinct examples.
- Rotate codebooks while retaining readable, dated older marks.
- Optionally hide marks as radiation signatures that require scanning.
- The discussed design used predefined symbols and in-game decryption.

## Asteroid mirages

Original proposal: [GitHub issue #495](https://github.com/jsolly/GeoRoids/issues/495).
Former task: [Todoist](https://app.todoist.com/app/task/6hRXX2vwHGXpMGM2).

- Show distant projections of real, valuable asteroids in unexpected regions.
- Reward observing or scanning an undisturbed projection with a bearing to its
  physical source.
- Let shooting the projection destroy the clue without granting mining rewards.
- Distinguish projections through unusually strong radiation signatures.

Reflective asteroids, spinning/tether mechanics, and automatic client refresh
remain active release work and are not parked by this decision.
